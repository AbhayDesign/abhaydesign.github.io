AstroidPong

Pong meets Asteroids. Survive the rounds.

LIVES
Aim: A / D or arrow keys. Fire: Space. Pause: P.

The goal

Two CPUs are playing Pong and you are the ship in the middle. Shoot the balls before they reach you. Each ball you destroy scores 100 points and a new one is served.

The rounds

Each round lasts 10 seconds. After every round one more ball joins the game, so round 5 has 5 balls. Finish a round for a bonus.

The controls

Rotate with A D or the arrow keys, fire with Space, pause with P. You have 3 lives, and on touch screens use the buttons.
